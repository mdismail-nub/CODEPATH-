## 2026-06-24 - Tactile Feedback and Copy Code Micro-UX
**Learning:** Adding `active:scale-95` to primary buttons and interactive elements significantly improves the perceived responsiveness and quality of the UI. Combining this with clear accessibility markers (ARIA labels/expanded states) and functional improvements (Copy Code) creates a more "delightful" experience.
**Action:** Consistently apply `active:scale-95` for tactile feedback and ensure all icon-only or toggleable buttons have appropriate ARIA attributes.
