## 2025-05-14 - Standardizing Tactile Feedback and Accessibility

**Learning:** Consistent tactile feedback via `active:scale-95` improves the perceived responsiveness of the app, and explicit `aria-label` attributes on icon-only buttons are essential for screen reader users, even when tooltips are present.

**Action:** Apply `active:scale-95` to all interactive elements and ensure all icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label` attributes.
